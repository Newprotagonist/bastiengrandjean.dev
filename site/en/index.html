Cook It Green

A vegan meal planner that works out, to the gram, how much of each ingredient a recipe needs to hit nutrition targets.

A 16-week annual rotation of 48 dishes, cooked in one 3-hour batch session per week. For each recipe, a solver adjusts three levers (protein, carbs, fat) to meet each user’s targets. The app also generates the weekly plan, the shopping list and a run sheet for the cooking session. Designed and built on my own, from product to infrastructure.

Technical Highlights

  • Bounded search over the grid of ingredient weights (5 g steps), penalising shortfalls 40 times more than excess
  • All 48 dishes recalculated in about 800 ms when a target changes, with targets and constants stored in the database
  • Needs calculation (Mifflin-St Jeor, Katch-McArdle) from Ciqual (ANSES) and USDA reference data
  • Domain logic in dedicated Ruby services (Nutrition::Solver, Menu::Recalculator…)
  • Multi-user PWA with Web Push reminders, GitHub Actions CI, Kamal deployment on Hetzner
